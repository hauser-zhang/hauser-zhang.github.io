About

I am currently a Ph.D. candidate at the Academy for Advanced Interdisciplinary Studies (Future Technology Institute), Peking University, where I focus on AI-driven genomics and proteomics research. My research interests lie at the intersection of artificial intelligence and biological sciences, particularly in developing computational methods for analyzing large-scale genomic and proteomic data. I am also deeply interested in large language models (LLMs) inference acceleration, training optimization, and autonomous agent systems. I'm always open to research collaboration and academic exchange. If you are interested in discussing related topics, feel free to reach out via email.
